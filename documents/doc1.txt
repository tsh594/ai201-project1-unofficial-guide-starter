Topic: The Flats at University
Review: I live at The Flats right now and it's alright. It's pretty pricey but basically equivalent to getting a single apartment on campus. I would say if you have people you want to room with try looking at other apartments first. But if you don't know anyone you want to room with, The Flats can assign you random roommates since they charge by individual room and not by the entire unit. I will say though management (like all landlords) aren't too fun to deal with. Luckily I personally haven't had a terrible experience but since it's a newer building they're still working out some technical kinks with appliances.
