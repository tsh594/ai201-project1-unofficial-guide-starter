Topic: The Flats Leasing Terms
Review: The Flats does have pretty nice accommodations from what I've heard from friends, as they specifically pair you with roommates who are also going to GMU based on a questionnaire profile. Crucially, they allow you to sign an academic-year lease only (running from August to May) instead of forcing you into a standard 12-month commercial lease. This saves you thousands if you plan to go back home for summer.
