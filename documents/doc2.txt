Topic: King's Park West Townhouses
Review: As far as locations go, I highly recommend trying to find a room in a townhouse in King's Park West. The neighborhood is directly across the street from GMU, making getting to campus ridiculously easy. I can make it from my bedroom to my office in under 15 minutes walking, and in under 5 minutes driving. That's huge for a graduate student or anyone who needs to be on campus from 9 to 5 most weekdays. Overall, I'd say being so close to campus has made my semesters a lot easier to handle.
