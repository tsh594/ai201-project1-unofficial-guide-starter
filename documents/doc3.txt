Topic: Budget Townhouse Living
Review: It completely depends on where you stay and what you are looking for! I was sick of expensive on-campus housing prices while also sharing a small room with a roommate, so I went off campus. I lucked out majorly by staying in a townhouse with 3 other girls with rent only $550 a month. We have a kitchen, laundry room, and living room, which is super nice, and I stay in my own private bedroom. Apartments are usually way more expensive, ranging anywhere from $1500 to $2500+ if you try to live alone. If you are fine with living with others, townhouses are the best option.
