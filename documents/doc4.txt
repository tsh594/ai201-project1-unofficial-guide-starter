Topic: The Main vs The Flats
Review: I currently live at The Main on University but I do not recommend it at all due to ongoing management issues and noise. If you are looking into student-targeted off-campus housing, check out The Flats on University instead. The rent depends on how many roommates you have. Rooms start from a 4-bedroom 4-bathroom setup all the way up to a 1-bedroom 1-bathroom layout. It just depends on what you can afford.
