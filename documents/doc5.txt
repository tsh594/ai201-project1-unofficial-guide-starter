Topic: Rent Market Trends in Fairfax
Review: Check the 'GMU Off-Campus Housing' Facebook marketplace group. Three years ago, rent for a single room in a shared house was in the $500 to $800 range. Now it is regularly pushing $850 to $1200. It's honestly getting crazy how fast rent has increased around Fairfax. Everyone I know is using Facebook or Nextdoor to find local homeowners renting out single basement rooms just to keep costs under $1000.
