Topic: Trillium Apartments (Formerly Circle Towers)
Review: Living at Trillium Apartments, formerly known as Circle Towers, has turned into an absolute nightmare. Initially discovering this apartment complex through George Mason University's off-campus housing portal led me to believe it would be a secure and suitable living option for a student like me. Little did I know that it would be an ordeal that would leave me feeling constantly on edge. The building safety features frequently break, and management response times are terrible. Discovering that George Mason University loosely endorses this apartment complex on its portal for its students is profoundly disappointing.
