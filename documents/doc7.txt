Topic: Camden Fairfax Corner Deliveries
Review: If you move to Camden at Fairfax Corner, never expect easy package or food deliveries to your apartment door. The complex layout is confusing and your specific address isn't going to be enough for anyone's GPS map to accurately find your door. You will almost always have to walk half a mile to the main community building entrance just to meet a delivery person or food courier.
