Topic: Fairfax Square Apartments
Review: Fairfax Square was amazing if you didn't live on the ground floor. Ground floor units have minor issues with pests and lack of natural sunlight because of the way the structural landscaping is done outside the windows. If you get an upper floor unit and split it with roommates, the floorplans are massive compared to what you get on campus for the exact same price.
