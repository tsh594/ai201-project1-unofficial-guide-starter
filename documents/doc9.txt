Topic: Townhouse vs Campus Apartment Costs
Review: My rent for a 12-month lease in a local townhouse is roughly the same total cost as a traditional double dorm on campus for just the school year. Going off campus doesn't cost me extra, I get way more physical space, and I don't have to pack up and move my stuff out during winter break or summer terms. The main downside is losing the extreme convenience of walking 5 minutes to class or the dining hall.
